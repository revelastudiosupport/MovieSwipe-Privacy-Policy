MovieSwipe Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains how MovieSwipe ("MovieSwipe", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the MovieSwipe mobile application and related services.

Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • Movie activity and preferences: movies you like, dislike, save, or mark as watched; selected genres, streaming providers, and region.
  • Feature content: prompts you submit to AI-powered discovery and a display name, room code, and votes when you use Movie Night.
  • Purchase information: subscription status and purchase history supplied by Apple, together with an app-specific user identifier used to provide paid features and restore purchases.
  • Usage data: interactions with the app, device and operating-system information, app version, and approximate technical information such as IP address.
  • Advertising data (free version only): a device identifier for vendors (IDFV), ad views and taps, and the technical information listed above, processed by our advertising partner to show and measure ads. See "Advertising" below.

MovieSwipe does not request precise location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate and personalize movie discovery, synchronize your private movie lists, provide Movie Night and AI-powered discovery, manage free-use limits and subscriptions, show ads in the free version, restore purchases, prevent abuse, maintain security and reliability, understand feature usage, and improve the app.

Services That Process Information

We use service providers only where needed to operate MovieSwipe:

  • Apple iCloud and CloudKit to synchronize private app data and support Movie Night.
  • RevenueCat to manage subscription entitlements and purchase status.
  • Firebase Analytics to understand aggregated app usage and improve features.
  • Cloudflare to host and protect backend services.
  • OpenAI to process prompts submitted to AI-powered discovery.
  • XMDb and movie-data providers to retrieve movie information and availability.
  • Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited) to show ads in the free version and to measure them and prevent ad fraud.

These providers process information under their own privacy terms and our service arrangements. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users or our rights, or in connection with a business transfer.

Advertising

The free version of MovieSwipe shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). MovieSwipe Pro subscribers do not see ads, and no ads are requested for them. Two kinds of ads appear: a full-screen ad shown occasionally while you swipe, and an optional ad you can choose to watch in exchange for extra swipes.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect your IP address, the identifier for vendors (IDFV), device and app information such as model, operating system and language, interactions with ads such as views and taps, and your approximate location derived from your IP address.

MovieSwipe does not request access to Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, MovieSwipe asks for your choice before any ad is requested, using Google's consent form (which follows the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework). If you consent, Google may use this information to personalise ads. If you do not consent, Google may still show limited, non-personalised ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time under Settings > Ad privacy settings in MovieSwipe. The legal basis for personalised ads is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR); withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before.

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services, the Google Privacy Policy, and My Ad Center to manage ads personalisation in your Google account.

No Sale or Cross-App Tracking

We do not sell personal information. MovieSwipe does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not link information from MovieSwipe with information from other companies' apps or websites to track you. Our advertising partner's use of information is described under "Advertising" above.

Storage and Retention

Movie activity and preferences are retained while needed to provide the service, until you reset or delete the relevant data, remove it from iCloud, or ask us to delete it. Movie Night rooms are designed to expire after 24 hours. Purchase records may be retained by Apple and RevenueCat as needed to provide subscriptions and meet legal obligations. Analytics, advertising and technical records are retained according to the applicable service provider's retention settings and only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can change streaming preferences and other app settings at any time.
  • You can turn notifications off in iOS Settings.
  • You can change or withdraw your consent for ads under Settings > Ad privacy settings (shown where consent is required). Subscribing to MovieSwipe Pro removes ads entirely.
  • You can use Settings > Delete My Data in MovieSwipe to delete the private movie records covered by that control.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple account's subscription settings.
  • You can stop further collection by uninstalling the app.

For access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests relating to information not covered by the in-app control, contact us at movieswipecontact@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request.

Children

MovieSwipe is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

International Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as MovieSwipe changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. Material changes will be communicated where required.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email movieswipecontact@gmail.com.
